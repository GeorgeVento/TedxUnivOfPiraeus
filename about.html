The Global Movement

What is TEDx?

TED is a nonprofit devoted to spreading ideas, usually in the form of short, powerful talks. TED began in 1984 as a conference where Technology, Entertainment and Design converged, and today covers almost all topics — from science to business to global issues — in more than 100 languages.

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience.

"At TED, we believe passionately in the power of ideas to change attitudes, lives and, ultimately, the world."

Our Story

TEDxUniversity
of Piraeus

At the University of Piraeus, we believe that ideas have the power to transform communities, industries, and lives. TEDxUniversityofPiraeus was born out of a desire to create a local platform where brilliant minds — students, academics, entrepreneurs, artists, and thinkers — could share their most powerful ideas.

Located in Piraeus, one of Europe's largest and most historic ports, we are uniquely positioned at the intersection of commerce, culture, and innovation. Our event reflects the spirit of this city: bold, dynamic, and always looking forward.

Every year, we curate a diverse lineup of speakers who challenge conventional thinking and offer fresh perspectives on the most pressing questions of our time.
