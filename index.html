In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience.

TEDxUniversityofPiraeus is an independently organized TEDx event at the heart of one of Greece's most dynamic universities. We create a platform for thinkers, innovators, and storytellers to share ideas that challenge, inspire, and transform.

Our 2026 edition invites you to explore a radical concept: what happens when we stop rushing? When we give ourselves the time and space to think clearly, act purposefully, and live fully?
